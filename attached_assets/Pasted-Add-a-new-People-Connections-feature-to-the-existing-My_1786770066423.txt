Add a new “People & Connections” feature to the existing My Day AI app.

The purpose is to help me stay connected with important people because I can lose track of time and unintentionally forget to reach out. This should feel like a gentle relationship assistant, not a CRM or another task list.

Core features

1. People

• Let me add specific people I want to stay connected with.
• Store name, relationship, preferred contact method, desired contact frequency, birthday/important dates, and optional notes.
• Do NOT import my entire contact list automatically.

2. Connection tracking
Make logging extremely easy:

• “I talked to Mom today.”
• “I saw Sarah yesterday.”
• “I just called my sister.”

The AI should recognize the person and interaction and automatically update their last connection date.

Also provide a one-tap Log Connection button.

3. Smart reminders
Allow me to choose a preferred connection rhythm (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, custom).

Reminders should say:

> “It’s been a little while since you connected with Sarah. Want to check in?”

Never use guilt-based language such as “overdue,” “you forgot,” or “you failed.”

Account for upcoming calendar events. If I have dinner with Sarah tomorrow, don’t also remind me to call her.

4. AI integration
The existing AI assistant should understand commands such as:

• “Who should I check in with?”
• “When did I last talk to Mom?”
• “Remind me to call Sarah next weekend.”
• “I haven’t talked to Lisa in forever.”
• “I just talked to my sister.”

Create structured AI actions/tools where appropriate, such as:

• create_person
• log_connection
• get_last_connection
• get_people_to_check_in_with
• create_connection_reminder
• save_person_note
• get_upcoming_birthdays

5. Reconnection help
If I say:

> “I haven’t talked to Sarah in months.”

The AI should respond warmly and offer:

• Draft a casual text
• Call
• Remind me later
• Not now

The AI can use saved notes/context to personalize the suggested message.

6. Important dates
Track birthdays and optional important dates and provide gentle reminders.

7. Anti-nagging
If I repeatedly dismiss a reminder, don’t keep nagging me. Offer:

• Remind me less often
• Change reminder time
• Remind me monthly
• Stop reminding me

8. Calendar integration
Use existing calendar functionality to recognize upcoming interactions with people and avoid duplicate reminders.

UX requirements

Keep this feature extremely simple and fast.

The goal is:

Think of someone → tell the AI → connection handled.

Do NOT create relationship scores, streaks, productivity points, or guilt-based tracking.

The feature should feel warm, elegant, private, and human.

Add a People/Connections section to the existing navigation and a subtle “Stay Connected” card on the home screen when relevant.

Important

Do not rebuild the existing app.

First inspect the current architecture and reuse the existing AI, database, calendar, reminders, voice, and notification systems wherever possible.

Implement this as an integrated capability of My Day AI, not as a separate mini-app.
